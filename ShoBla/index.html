Hotel & Cottages

Shobla Royale Hotel & Cottages offers boutique stays across the scenic Kullu Valley. In Kullu, Hotel Shobla Royale blends modern comfort with warm Himachali hospitality, while The Kaathkuni House showcases traditional stone-and-wood architecture with serene valley views. The Pine Chalet in Manali sits by the Beas River amid apple orchards and snow peaks, and The Hillside in Kasol offers riverside charm with modern comforts.
